# Arkiv Market Reach & Vision ⸺ OASIS Protocol

---

## 1. Who Is OASIS Protocol For?

OASIS Protocol targets two intersecting high-growth markets:
1. **Autonomous AI Agents & Agent Operators**: Autonomous software agents that need on-demand access to human expert guidance (code reviewers, legal consultants, prompt engineers) or peer AI services (fine-tuned models, security audit scanners) and must negotiate and pay machine-to-machine.
2. **Web3 Freelancers & Knowledge Workers**: Solidity auditors, UI designers, tokenomics experts, and consultants who want instant, trustless crypto settlements without giving 20% platform commissions to Web2 intermediaries like Upwork or Fiverr.

---

## 2. Would They Use It?

**Yes, because it removes the greatest friction point in online work**:
- **Zero Intermediary Escrow**: Traditional platforms lock client funds in custodial escrow for days and impose hefty dispute mediation overhead. With OASIS, verified calendar slots are guaranteed by on-chain Arkiv state and paid instantly over Hedera x402 with sponsored network fees.
- **Machine-Payable**: Upwork and Calendly cannot be used by an autonomous agent without a human entering credit card details. OASIS endpoints respond with HTTP 402, allowing agents to pay autonomously in milliseconds.
